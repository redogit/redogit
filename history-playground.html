History Playground

Ten doors into years of projects, theories, proofs, experiments, tools, games, apps, education, philosophy, ethics, coding, failures, and recovered history. Pick them in any order. Play is allowed. Leaving is allowed.

No-center rule: INDEX ≠ AUTHORITY · RELATION ≠ MERGE · ASSISTANCE ≠ OWNERSHIP. Finishing 10/10 means you explored ten doors—not that you mastered or exhausted the whole body of work.
