0 // Terms first

WISDOM := a rule / relation / warning / method earned through consequence, comparison, correction, and reuse.
CONDENSED WISDOM := a small operational carrier that still has a way Homeward to what earned it.
EXPERIENCE := observations + actions + consequences + failures + repairs + context. Experience != universal evidence.
HOMEWARD := reverse path from the short rule to conditions, exceptions, evidence, obligations, and source.
KNOWLEDGE DECAY := the words remain but a consequential distinction needed to know when / why / whether to apply them is no longer reconstructible.

7 // Current proof-work example

DON'T BRANCH ON A DEGREE
UNTIL I HAVE FAILED TO PROVE THAT THE DEGREE IS:

    REDUNDANT,
    DOMINATED,
    FUNCTIONALLY DETERMINED,
    LOCALLY SATISFIED,
    OR CHEAPLY PROJECTABLE.

The sentence is only useful because I can unfold it back into the exact rules, guards, receipts, lifecycle cost, and remainder that earned it.

THE RULE IS THE CARRIER.
THE PROOF / TRACE / CONDITIONS ARE WHY I AM ALLOWED TO TRUST THE CARRIER.

8 // Claim ceiling

This is a current research / engineering method for preserving and compressing reusable operational knowledge. It is not a theorem that all human wisdom is algorithmically extractable, that the compression is unique or optimal, or that repeated usefulness establishes universal validity.

WHEN I COMPRESS A LEARNED RULE,
KEEP ENOUGH OF WHY IT WORKED
THAT I CAN QUESTION IT,
RECONSTRUCT IT,
REPAIR IT,
AND KNOW WHEN NOT TO USE IT.
