0 // DEFINITION OF TERMS — I NEED THESE WORDS BEFORE I DO ANYTHING ELSE

I need to mean the same thing when I reuse a word later. If the word moves, the proof can look like it moved when really the language did.

RESULT := what came out. Not automatically method, proof, explanation, or reusable knowledge.
METHOD := the repeatable relation between starting state, lawful moves, checks, cost, and result. One successful execution != Method.
CONDENSED WISDOM := a compressed operational rule that can still be unfolded into the experience, failures, conditions, evidence, and remainder that earned it.
OBLIGATION := what the result actually has to satisfy. Not the story I hope the solver tells.
OBJECT := the thing I am preserving or reasoning about. Not its current filename, coordinate, encoding, wording, or carrier.
SURFACE := the current view / address / interface where an Object is available. Failed representation != failed Object.
CARRIER := the structure currently holding or moving what matters. When it starts losing recoverability, move the carrier rather than letting the knowledge decay with it.
CONTEXT := the relations that make a meaning valid here. Contextual equivalence != universal equivalence.
RELATION := an explicit typed connection. Not resemblance, adjacency, or co-occurrence.
CONSEQUENTIAL DISTINCTION := a difference whose resolution can change what I may lawfully do next.
ONE-DEGREE MOVE := change one consequential degree while holding the rest fixed enough to know what caused the result.
TRACE := the ordered record of what I selected, changed, checked, observed, and retained. Trace != proof by itself.
REMAINDER := what is still unresolved after exact work is removed. Remainder != failure.
HOMEWARD := the way back from a transformed / compressed / solved carrier to the original obligation, witness, and source.
RECONSTRUCTION := recovering what mattered about an earlier state from preserved relations, receipts, and provenance. Summary != source.
KNOWLEDGE DECAY := a consequential distinction that used to be reconstructible is no longer reconstructible.
EVIDENCE := what was actually observed, proved, checked, or independently supported.
AUTHORITY := what is allowed to control the current decision. Newest != automatically authoritative.
PROVENANCE := where this came from, what changed it, and what evidence supports the change.
CLAIM CEILING := the strongest claim the current evidence actually supports.

1 // THE DOUGHNUT LAW

MAKING THE DOUGHNUTS
!=
KNOWING HOW TO MAKE THE DOUGHNUTS
!=
PRESERVING WHY / WHEN / FOR WHOM THE DOUGHNUTS MATTER

I can make a thing once and still lose the knowledge that made it repeatable. I can preserve a recipe and still lose the reason the recipe existed. I can preserve the words and still lose the relations needed to reconstruct what they meant.

RESULT != METHOD != PROOF != MEANING.
Carrier != Object. Description != Reconstruction.
