## 1. Falsification signatures and the semantic intersection semilattice

For every canonical non-tautological clause C, let sig(C) be the partial assignment that makes every literal of C false.

Two signatures are compatible exactly when they never assign opposite values to the same variable. For compatible signatures a,b define a join b as their union.

Let L(F) be the smallest set containing the empty signature and every sig(C), and closed under every defined compatible join.

Each beta in L(F) denotes the exact subcube

## 2. Clique-to-state quotient theorem

Let Q_F be the clause nonclash graph.

For every nonempty clause set T:

    intersection_{C in T} Bad(C) = E_beta_T

Define

Then L(F) without the empty state is exactly the quotient of nonempty conformal-clique descriptions by equality of their intersection event.

## 3. Möbius exact count

Order L(F) by extension of partial assignments:

    alpha <= beta iff alpha subseteq beta.

Let

    mu(empty)=1

and for nonempty beta,

    mu(beta) = - sum_{alpha < beta} mu(alpha).

Then

    #SAT(F)
      =
    sum_{beta in L(F)}
      mu(beta) * 2^(n-|dom(beta)|).

Proof: ordinary inclusion-exclusion sums over all compatible clause subsets. Group all subsets that produce the same intersection subcube. Their total signed coefficient is exactly the Möbius coefficient of that distinct intersection in the intersection poset.

This is the standard intersection-poset form of inclusion-exclusion / Möbius inversion, specialized here to Boolean subcubes. External alignment: Richard Stanley, Enumerative Combinatorics, Vol. 1, Sections 3.7 and 3.11.

## 7. Strict separation from raw clique multiplicity

For odd p=2q+1, let r=q+1 and build F_p from:

- every positive r-clause on the p variables;
- every negative r-clause on the same variables.

Let

    M = binomial(p,r).

There are 2M clauses.

Same-sign clauses form two separate K_M components in Q_F. Every positive/negative cross pair clashes because two r-subsets of a p-set must intersect when 2r>p.

Therefore

    K(F_p)=2(2^M-1).

Raw clique descriptions are exponential in the clause count.

But a positive-side semantic event is determined only by the union U of variables forced false, and exactly every U with |U|>=r occurs. The negative side is symmetric, and cross-sign joins are impossible.

Hence

    J(F_p)
      =
    1 + 2 * sum_{j=r}^p binomial(p,j)
      =
    1 + 2^p.

Since M >= 2^p/(p+1),

    J(F_p) <= 1+(p+1)M,

polynomial in the encoded input size.

Every F_p is UNSAT because every assignment has at least r true variables or at least r false variables, falsifying one negative or positive r-clause respectively.

The signed clause matrix also has full column rank and a strictly positive balance vector; opposite signed rows form positive 2-circuits. So this separation directly intersects the active balance carrier.
