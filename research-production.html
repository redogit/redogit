Research + Production

A switchboard for questions, experiments, tools, apps, games, teaching, and recovered history. Research can inspire production. Production can generate new questions. Neither silently upgrades the other.

Firewall: RESEARCH RESULT ≠ PRODUCT FEATURE · PRODUCT SUCCESS ≠ SCIENTIFIC VALIDATION · PLAY ≠ EVIDENCE · TEACHING ≠ AUTHORITY TRANSFER.

Keep the bridge removable

If a product depends on a research result, pin the exact dependency and provide a fallback where practical. If a product becomes popular, do not use adoption as proof. If a combined view becomes cognitively heavy, split the view before centralizing the projects.
