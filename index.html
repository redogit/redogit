The central idea behind my work is that higher organization does not require sameness: differentiation + relation + coordination. Growth means preserving a valid lineage and important existing capability while opening new, safely coordinatable possibilities.

REDOGIT: preserve the predecessor, make the difference explicit, verify what can actually be verified, and continue without rewriting history.

About me

The technical we

I follow the pattern outward: bounded self-maintaining cells → organisms → animals in environments → social coordination → language and culture → technical extension → distributed human-machine cognition → civilization → planetary coupling → meta-coordination → collective self-modeling.

I do not treat these levels as literally identical. The question is structural: how can different components preserve agency, boundaries, provenance, and evidence while coordinating strongly enough to sense, distinguish, remember, model, value, act, repair, learn, and reorganize together?

Adaptive organization is the ability to modify organization while preserving enough continuity to remain meaningfully the same evolving system. A strong growth step preserves protected capability, adds genuine capability, and stays inside explicit safety and evidence boundaries.

The technical we becomes especially interesting when the coordinated whole can do something its members could not do separately without erasing what the members could already do. Coordination is not homogenization. Differentiation is part of the capability.
