## 2. Monochromatic-edge events

For every hyperedge e define

    Mono(e)
      =
    { colorings in which every vertex of e has the same color }.

Then:

    proper 2-coloring of H
      iff
    coloring avoids every Mono(e).

For A subseteq E, intersecting the events Mono(e), e in A, does not fix a truth value.

It imposes only equality:

    all vertices joined through selected hyperedges
    must receive the same color.

Let pi_A be the partition of V into connected components of the selected-edge subhypergraph (isolated vertices included as singleton blocks).

Then:

    intersection_{e in A} Mono(e)

depends only on pi_A, and its size is

    2^(|pi_A|).

Thus many edge-subset histories may denote one exact equality event.

## 6. Homeward witness without rebuilding the carrier

Retain L_H and its Möbius coefficients.

Otherwise let

    fixed_alpha(pi)

be the number of blocks of pi containing at least one alpha-assigned vertex.

Then the number of full colorings extending alpha and lying in the equality event pi is

    2^(|pi|-fixed_alpha(pi)).

    #proper extensions of alpha
